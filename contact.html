Frequently Asked Contact Questions

How long does it take for a developer submission to be reviewed?
Our QA team reviews developer submissions within 3 to 5 business days. If approved and benchmarked at 60 FPS, your title is added to our catalog with full author attribution.
What should I do if a game gets stuck on the loading screen?
First, try clicking the Reload button right below the game screen. If it still does not load, ensure third-party cookies or strict ad-blocker filters are not blocking WebGL canvases, then submit a bug report through the form above.
Do you charge any fee to play or submit games?
Never. Low's Adventures is 100% free for players and 100% free for indie game developers who want their titles curated for our audience.
