Editorial Policy & Game Curation

Transparency, technical rigor, and player safety form the bedrock of Low's Adventures. Learn how our editorial team benchmarks, tests, and certifies every game before it enters our public catalog.

Version 2.4 Authored by Alex Nguyen, Editor-in-Chief Effective: 2026

1. Editorial Independence

At Low's Adventures, our editorial recommendations, ratings, and inclusions are 100% independent. We do not accept sponsored placements, paid reviews, or financial incentives from publishers to boost a title's ranking or feature a game that fails our safety metrics.

Core Principle: If a game lags, crashes on low-spec hardware, displays predatory ad pop-ups, or compromises student safety, it is immediately rejected or de-listed — regardless of its popularity.

2. The 5-Stage Game Testing Pipeline

Every single game submitted or reviewed for Low's Adventures undergoes a five-stage evaluation before being published:

  1. Source Code & Network Security Audit: Our security engineers inspect the game package to ensure zero coin-miners, zero hidden tracking pixels, and zero redirects to external malicious domains.
  2. Hardware Benchmark: The game is tested on a range of devices (e.g. Intel Celeron Chromebook, modern Core i5 laptop, Apple Silicon Mac, and standard Android/iOS mobile browsers).
  3. Frame-rate & Memory Profiling: We monitor CPU usage, memory leaks, and frame rate stability over a continuous 30-minute play session to prevent browser tab crashes.
  4. Input & Accessibility Check: Controls must be responsive, clearly labeled, and compatible with keyboard arrows, WASD keys, or standard touchscreen gestures.
  5. Content & Age-Appropriateness Review: We verify that the game is suitable for school environments and free of graphic violence, profanity, or simulated gambling.

3. Performance & 60 FPS Benchmarks

Lag destroys arcade enjoyment. To meet our benchmark certification:

  • Initial Asset Load: Initial game files must load in under 3.5 seconds on a standard 15 Mbps broadband connection.
  • Render Consistency: Games must maintain a median frame rate of at least 50 FPS during complex canvas animations or 3D WebGL scenes.
  • Audio Engine: Sound effects and background music must be volume-balanced and easily mutable via in-game controls or browser tab muting.

4. Minor Safety & COPPA Adherence

A massive percentage of our community consists of students playing during lunch hours or recreational study periods. Therefore, we strictly follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) guidelines:

  • No personal identification data (names, emails, geolocation) is requested before playing.
  • No chat systems with unfiltered open communication are integrated without active moderation.
  • Games do not link directly to predatory e-commerce stores or ask for credit card numbers.

5. How We Calculate Ratings & Badges

Each game card displays a rating (e.g., ⭐ 4.8) and a badge (e.g., HOT, TOP, or NEW). Here is how these are determined:

  • User Ratings: Aggregated from real community feedback, play session duration, and interactive favorites stored via browser storage.
  • Editorial Badges:
    • ★ LATEST: Brand new releases or recent updates from flagship series like Low's Adventures 3.
    • 🔥 HOT: Titles experiencing high community traffic and completion rates (e.g. Monkey Mart, Retro Bowl).
    • TOP: Top-tier games with over 90% positive player completion rates across 500+ votes.

6. Developer Submissions

We welcome independent HTML5 developers who want to showcase their creations to our global gaming audience. To submit a title:

  • Ensure the game is self-contained or hosted on an HTTPS-compliant domain.
  • Ensure the game does not show interstitial video ads longer than 5 seconds.
  • Send a play link and developer bio to submissions@lowsadventure.github.io or via our Contact Form.

7. Corrections & Update Frequency

If a game updates its levels, alters its controls, or if a bug is reported by our community, our QA team re-tests the build within 48 hours. Any game that becomes unstable or ceases to load is removed until the developer resolves the issue.
