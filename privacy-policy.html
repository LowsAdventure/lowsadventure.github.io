Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are our highest priorities. This document details how Low's Adventures minimizes data collection, protects student privacy under COPPA, and complies with international GDPR and CCPA standards.

1. Overview & Data Minimization

At Low's Adventures (accessible at https://lowsadventure.github.io), we believe the safest data is data that is never collected in the first place. You can play all 340+ games without creating an account, entering an email, or revealing your real-world identity.

We do NOT: sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information. We do not maintain marketing email lists or engage in behavioral cross-site user tracking.

2. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Because our web games are widely enjoyed by children, students, and teenagers in educational settings, we strictly adhere to the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • We do not require children to disclose more information than is reasonably necessary to participate in game activities.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that their child has sent personal data via our contact form without consent, please contact us at privacy@lowsadventure.github.io and we will permanently delete the information immediately.

3. LocalStorage & Browser Cookies

We use modern web localStorage purely to enhance your individual gameplay session on your device:

  • Game Level Progress: Games like Low's Adventures 1, 2, and 3 store level completions and coin counts locally on your computer.
  • Favorites & Likes: Storing which games you have liked (e.g. lows_liked key) so your heart icon remains filled on return visits.
  • Display Preferences: Remembering Theater Mode or dark mode preferences.

This data never leaves your browser and can be completely wiped at any time simply by clearing your browser's site data.

4. Third-Party Game Embeds & Sandboxing

Some games on Low's Adventures are delivered via secure HTML5 iframes hosted on verified partner domains (e.g., webglmath.github.io, slope-game.github.io, ubg77.github.io).

All iframes run inside modern browser sandboxes to protect you against cross-site scripting (XSS) and unauthorized local disk access. We audit these embeds regularly to verify they adhere to our zero-malware and zero-pop-under standard.

5. European Union (GDPR) Rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users within the EEA have specific rights regarding their data:

  • Right to Access & Rectification: You may request a copy of any communication you have sent to us.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request that any support ticket or correspondence be permanently erased.
  • Right to Object: You may object to any processing based on legitimate interests.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you the right to know what categories of personal information are collected and the right to opt out of the "sale" of personal data.

Declaration: Low's Adventures does not sell, share, or monetize consumer personal information.

7. Data Security Measures

All traffic between your device and Low's Adventures is strictly encrypted using HTTPS with TLS 1.3. Our static infrastructure is served via GitHub Pages with global CDN caching and automated DDoS mitigation.

8. Privacy Officer Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, our designated Data Protection Officer can be reached at:

Email: privacy@lowsadventure.github.io
Website: https://lowsadventure.github.io
